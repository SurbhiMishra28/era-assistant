The technology underneath

Built for real-time, everywhere conversation — and engineered so voice data never leaves your network unless you choose cloud processing.

🎙️

NVIDIA Sofia voice

Studio-grade TTS in English, Hindi, and Hinglish with streaming synthesis and natural lip-synced delivery.

⚡

Streaming STT

Real-time speech-to-text with on-device wake-word detection — Era starts listening before you finish saying her name.

🛡️

On-device processing

Wake-word, transcription, and intent handling run locally by default. Cloud relay is opt-in, encrypted in transit and at rest.

🧠

Contextual memory

A long-lived, patient-scoped memory engine that recalls visit history, preferences, and pending treatments across sessions.

🔗

Secure integration

Session-authenticated APIs with TLS termination, HTTPS-only flows, and SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure.

🌐

Deploys anywhere

Cloud, hybrid, or full on-prem. Desktop, tablet, and phone — the same Era at the front desk and on your commute.
